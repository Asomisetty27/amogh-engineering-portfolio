The problem

A lid for an Arduino UNO chassis that slides on and stays put with no screws or extra hardware.

What I built

  • A parametric OpenSCAD generator: chassis opening, lid and skirt thickness, and clearance are parameters.
  • Two closures from one file: grooves that ride rails on the chassis, or self-latching spring tabs that snap under an interior lip.
  • Wrap, bridged, and stealth variants, set up to print top face down with no supports.
