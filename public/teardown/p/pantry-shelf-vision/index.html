The problem

A food pantry needs to know which shelves are running low. A detector trained on retail shelves made confident mistakes on the pantry’s real shelves.

What I built

  • A 3-stage training pipeline: SKU-110K pretraining, a gap fine-tune, and a 15-frame on-site fine-tune.
  • The deployment model was chosen by a pre-registered ablation, with the decision rule fixed before results existed.
  • Region conditioning that removed every observed false positive without breaking cases that already worked.
  • Deployment on a Jetson Orin Nano with an Arduino Nicla Vision camera node.

Results

0.920mAP50, deployed model
0.898mAP50, COCO-only baseline

What I learned

In the live test, the staff digest reported “All 0 shelves OK” while every frame was being rejected by its own safety check. Three of the four defects never appeared with simulated frames. A monitoring system’s failures live in the seam between sensor and model.
