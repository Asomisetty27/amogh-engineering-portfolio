The problem

Theta works from a GPU’s own sensors. A cheap, independent temperature node gives a second opinion on inlet and board temperatures, and is the first step toward a standalone thermal resistance instrument.

What I built

  • Schematic, placement, board outline, and mounting holes in KiCad.
  • One onboard and two screw-terminal remote DS18B20 probes on a shared 1-Wire bus, a DHT22 header, an I2C expansion header, and a status LED.
  • A five-board roadmap where each revision adds one hard thing: next a bare ESP32-C3 with its own USB-C and LDO, then a 4-channel MAX31856 thermocouple board.
