The problem

Design a working pneumatic air motor and make every part by hand.

What I built

  • Parametric SolidWorks parts and assembly with mates and interference checks, and engineering drawings with GD&T.
  • Manual lathe and mill work: facing, turning, boring, threading, milling, drilling, broaching, with feeds and speeds per material.
  • Six parts: crank disk, cylinder, flywheel, frame, mainshaft, piston.
  • A screwdriver assembly in SolidWorks built around McMaster-Carr parts (bits, a neodymium magnet, spring pins, washers), with an assembly drawing packet.
  • Mill Project #1 (6061-T6 aluminum, rev B) and lathe part drawings with sections, tapped holes, and countersinks.
