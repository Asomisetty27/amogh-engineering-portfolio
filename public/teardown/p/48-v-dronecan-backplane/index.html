The problem

The team’s 12S heavy-lift quadcopter ran its power and CAN wiring through a hand-wired harness.

What I built

  • A 2-layer, 120 × 90 mm backplane in KiCad, ERC and DRC clean, replacing the harness.
  • Four independent 48 V pass-throughs sized per IPC-2221 on 2 oz copper for 14 A continuous and 30 A burst.
  • Dual-line CAN TVS protection and switchable 120 Ω bus terminations.
  • A written bring-up procedure and a dimensioned drawing for the mechanical team. Taken through fabrication and bring-up.
