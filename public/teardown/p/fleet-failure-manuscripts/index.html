The problem

Most GPU reliability work studies one fleet. I wanted to know which failure patterns hold across very different fleets and cooling regimes.

What I built

  • “Peer-Relative Thermal Resistance as a Fleet Health Signal”: the detection method and the Princeton blind validation.
  • “GPU Failures Remember Events, Not Trajectories”: a failure taxonomy across five production fleets (Titan, Summit, Marconi100, GWDG, and Princeton’s Della).
  • The five-fleet study is a meta-analysis of mostly public fleet data. Princeton is the one live deployment.

Results
