The problem

Take an audio signal all the way through digital and back: condition it, sample it, and rebuild it with a DAC we designed and fabricated ourselves.

What I built

  • Binary-weighted 4-bit DAC (LM1458, 1206 resistor ladder 80k / 40k / 20k / 10k with 10k feedback, ±12 V) on a 1 × 1 in 2-layer PCB.
  • Full chain: audio source, summing-amp level shifter, Arduino 10-bit ADC, our DAC, oscilloscope and speaker.
  • Nine-lab build including a Fusion 360 chassis, bench instrumentation, and hand and reflow soldering.

What I learned

The ADC loaded the source, fixed with a voltage-follower buffer. The bipolar audio fell outside the ADC’s 0 to 5 V window, fixed with a DC offset. Audible quantization distortion is a real limit of 4 bits, so we documented it instead of hiding it.
