The problem

Nine physical stages across capacitive, optical, electromagnetic, and RF domains, where any single failure stops the whole sequence.

What I built

  • Capacitive-touch input (5-note sequence, 2.2 MΩ RC), relay isolation, a ~580 V strobe, Schmitt-trigger optical detection with ambient rejection.
  • Solenoid actuation, a 555-timer metal detector, MOSFET electromagnet release, a tilt switch, and an I2C LCD.
  • Firmware that sequences all nine stages end to end.

On the bench

Results

9stages in one sequence
6failure modes root-caused
8,760 → 8,310 Hzmetal detector shift (5.1%)

What I learned

The MOSFET would not switch reliably. The logic was right; the Arduino on USB and the 9 V supply shared no ground. Separately, the metal detector false-triggered on oscillator drift until we replaced percent-change math with a fixed 150 Hz threshold, a 5-reading streak, and a 600 ms release delay.
