The problem

The aircraft needs a control link where latency matters most and a telemetry link where range matters most, both on license-free bands.

What I built

  • Silicon choice: SX1280 at 2.4 GHz with ExpressLRS for control, SX1262 at 915 MHz for MAVLink telemetry.
  • A worst-case link budget: +20 dBm transmit, 2 dBi antennas each end, 109.7 dB free-space loss at 3 km, giving about −85.7 dBm at a receiver rated near −108 dBm.
  • An RF board checklist for the custom PCB: matching network, antenna feed, and layout.
